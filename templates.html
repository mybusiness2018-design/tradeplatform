EN
Subject: {产品} supplier for {国家} importers — {公司}

Hi {姓名},

This is {公司} — we supply Muslim prayer pillows, gift boxes and 3C accessories to importers across the Middle East and Southeast Asia.

Why buyers choose us:
- Stable Ramadan stock, no out-of-stock in peak season
- FOB Ningbo, 30% deposit / 70% before shipment
- OEM & custom embroidery available

Could I send you our best-seller catalog and a sample quote?

Best,
{公司}
中文要点
首封冷开发信要点：一句话亮明身份+核心品类；用 3 条差异化（斋月库存稳、付款友好、可定制）降低信任门槛；结尾给一个低门槛动作（发目录/报价），不要直接要订单。
